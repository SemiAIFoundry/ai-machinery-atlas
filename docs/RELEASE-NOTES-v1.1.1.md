## The complete project

AI Machinery Atlas is a navigable 3D learning platform for understanding how the machinery of modern AI comes together: atoms and electronic states, semiconductor devices, logic and memory, chip design and fabrication, packaging and HBM, processing architectures, computers and networks, data-center and orbital infrastructure, executable software, model training and inference, and evaluation.

Selectable procedural assemblies connect a component field guide with mechanisms, formal equations, assumptions, contextual specifications, interfaces, tradeoffs, knowledge checks, and primary references. Guided journeys follow constraints across scales. Numerical labs let learners explore simplified quantitative models. The CRG computation-to-realization bridge connects computation intent, architecture, chip design, physical build, system operation, and evidence.

*The Ascent: A Journey Through Modern AI* remains the cinematic companion, tracing attention and early GPT systems toward the frontier and the open questions around AGI.

The following coverage was established in v1.1.0 and is retained in v1.1.1:

| Resource | Current coverage |
| --- | --- |

The expanded treatment follows wafer fabrication and process windows; design representation and physical realization; package assembly, interconnects, and HBM; CPUs, GPUs, XPUs, and custom silicon; capacity allocation and queues; power, cooling, networks, and global infrastructure; orbital scenarios; and the relationship between machine constraints and model execution. Formal KaTeX/MathML equations, typed connections, architecture specimen comparisons, source-dated evidence, and shareable navigation state support this exploration.

## Validation and scope

`npm test` passed for v1.1.1: TypeScript and production build; curriculum, scene, prerequisite, relationship, and provenance checks across all 210 lessons; 13 numerical invariant tests; and four portable asset, metadata, film, and license checks.

Browser QA carried forward from v1.1.0 covered formal equations, the CRG bridge and process route, the numerical workbench, Global and Compare views, the deployed atlas route, and *The Ascent*. Those results describe the prior feature release; they are not a claim of a new full browser QA pass for this metadata revision. The existing production build reports a large-chunk warning for the 3D and curriculum payload.

Geometry and numerical models are explanatory. Product specifications are configuration-specific. CRG's approximately 55-year computation-to-realization interval is attributed to Semi AI Foundry's research claim. The atlas does not establish a universal chip-design theorem, disclose GPT-6 Astra's private architecture or deployed hardware, or establish that AGI has been achieved.
