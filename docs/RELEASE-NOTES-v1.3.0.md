# AI Machinery Atlas v1.3.0

**[Live atlas](https://semiaifoundry.com/ai-atlas/)** · **[Inside the machinery](https://semiaifoundry.com/ai-atlas/connected.html)** · **[The Ascent](https://semiaifoundry.com/ai-atlas/ascent.html)** · **[Downloads](https://github.com/SemiAIFoundry/ai-machinery-atlas/releases/tag/v1.3.0)**

AI Machinery Atlas is a 3D learning platform connecting the machinery behind AI: atoms and electronic states, transistors and arithmetic, chip design and fabrication, packaging and HBM, processing architectures, infrastructure, executable software, model learning and useful applications. The CRG bridge connects computation intent, physical realization and verification evidence. Procedural assemblies, scientific explanations, formal equations, numerical labs, cited history and cinematic companions connect these scales.

Version 1.3.0 expands the 224-lesson v1.2.0 edition to **311 lessons, 82 branches, 21 guided paths, 24 numerical labs, 83 historical or dated milestones and 28 engineering experiences**. Earlier lesson identities and the broad physical-to-intelligence curriculum remain available. Science coverage contains **345 cards: 335 formal expressions and 10 identified prose relationships**.

## Expanded science and engineering

The manufacturing route adds 14 mechanism-specific process sequences and 84 authored stages, with cross-sections, optional spatial inspection and explicit specimen boundaries. An oxide-dependent long-channel cell connects geometry to logic, noise and timing acceptance. An actual RTL-to-routed FPGA artifact exposes synthesis, mapping, routing and bounded verification evidence.

Hardware experiences execute matrix arithmetic through address mapping, cache visibility, buffer ownership, DRAM commands, finite-credit network delivery, clock crossings and error correction. A joined memory workload carries qualified package/tier conditions into actual bytes, damaged codewords and checked arithmetic. A learned query projection passes through two integer machine tiles, with disclosed precision conversion and comparison against the original model calculation. Broader packaging, HBM/HBF, electrical and optical interconnect, custom-silicon, capacity, global infrastructure and orbital-compute coverage is retained.

A seeded **423-parameter causal decoder** exposes forward tensors, reverse-mode gradients, optimizer updates, checkpoint/resume, continued pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, preference learning and a bounded reward objective. Data construction makes source families, train-only byte-pair fitting, packed targets and leakage visible. Distributed updates carry owned gradient shards through owner-specific optimization and replica reconstruction; committed checkpoints enter KV serving or a factory job with actual failed-checkpoint recovery.

Learned retrieval and generated proposals connect to typed actions, exact approval, durable journals, retries and independently checked fictional outcomes. Application experiences include exposure-aware ranking, forecasting, code-contract tests and closed-loop control. Contrasting learning families retain their distinct data, objectives, algorithms and evaluation boundaries.

## Learning delivery and reusable work

Lifecycle and discipline discovery distinguish explanations, worked examples, executable mechanisms and connected artifacts. Grouped experience selection, readable narrow layouts, optional 3D, formal mathematics and labeled controls support access across screen sizes. Saved experiments retain model/source identity, explicit restoration and recoverable earlier records.

The instructor pack preserves 14 original scenario contracts and adds three connected sessions, prediction worksheets, scoped answers and nine executable contract groups. The new **Inside the machinery** companion presents ten actual captured interface moments in 60 seconds, with chapter links, captions, transcript and an original instrumental score. It joins the original navigation walkthrough and *The Ascent*. Captures retain their original build identities; they are edited teaching sequences rather than uninterrupted recordings of the release build.

## Validation and continuing review

The preserved implementation validation recorded **729 passing tests**, **2,117 scientific checks**, complete curriculum/geometry checks, 14 curation tests, 14 original teaching contracts and nine additional contract groups. Selected desktop and 320 px browser tasks exercised source leakage, cell timing, distributed barriers, KV serving, learned proposals, memory faults, checkpoint restoration, ranking, network delivery and projection precision. The [release preflight](release-evidence/v1.3.0/preflight.log) ran after a fresh locked dependency install, with two concurrent test workers: 732 tests passed, plus curation and teaching-contract checks. [Five focused companion checks](release-evidence/v1.3.0/companion.log) also passed after moving chapter controls to external scripts for the production content security policy. Historical build identities and browser scope remain in the [validation record](roadmap/next-horizon/VALIDATION.md).

As of 7 September 2026, the [curation queue](curation/curation-review-queue.json) contains **21 current complete lesson source-scope reviews and 290 lessons requiring baseline review**. The latest 16-lesson batch recovered the original HBM source attribution and corrected a battery-energy boundary. Separate experience-specific checks do not silently approve entire lessons. Human specialist, physical iOS/Android, assistive-technology and 6–9 learner observations remain open. Coverage and automated checks do not establish complete scientific review, learning effectiveness or production hardware performance.
