# Licensing transition — 2026-09-06

Beginning with v1.2.0, AI Machinery Atlas is offered under the AI Machinery
Atlas Research and Noncommercial License 1.0. It follows the CRG Systems
scheme: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 base terms with attribution and explicit
commercial-use conditions. The restrictions cover atlas-owned software and
original content together. Academic, public-interest research, personal and
hobby use remain permitted. Commercial use requires a separate written
agreement from Semi AI Foundry, LLC.

The change applies to this distribution and future work released under these
terms. Third-party licenses remain unchanged. This license is source-available,
not an OSI-approved open-source license.

The earlier v1.0.0, v1.1.0 and v1.1.1 releases were distributed under MIT.
Their GitHub release pages, release tags and packaged assets were withdrawn
at the publisher's direction. Website download copies are removed with the
v1.2.0 deployment. Withdrawal does not cancel permissions already granted
under MIT for earlier copies, and the project makes no representation that
there were no prior downloads, forks or copies. Historical source commits and
their notices remain part of the repository record; they are not silently
relabelled under the new terms.

The additional Atlas conditions clarify that academic status or public funding
does not authorize commercial exploitation, preserve ordinary academic teaching,
and require the full custom license and attribution notice to travel with
redistributions. The PolyForm base text is preserved. No upstream dependency,
third-party research, factual information or trademark is relicensed.
