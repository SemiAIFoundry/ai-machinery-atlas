# AI Machinery Atlas v1.1.0

**[Live demo](https://semiaifoundry.com/ai-atlas/)** · **[Watch The Ascent](https://semiaifoundry.com/ai-atlas/ascent.html)** · **[Changelog](https://github.com/SemiAIFoundry/ai-machinery-atlas/blob/v1.1.0/CHANGELOG.md)**

Compatible expansion of the September 6, 2026 v1.0.0 reference release by Semi AI Foundry, LLC. The prior v1.0.0 tag and its downloadable artifacts remain immutable.

## Project scope retained

The atlas continues to teach the complete path from atoms and electronic states to semiconductor devices, logic, memory, packages, computers, networks, data-center and orbital infrastructure, executable software, model training and inference, and the evaluation questions surrounding advanced AI. It is a navigable learning platform rather than a vendor catalog or a claim about a private system. The Ascent film remains the cinematic companion for the historical arc from attention and early GPT systems toward the frontier and the open question of AGI.

## v1.1.0 coverage addendum

- Deepened the atomic-to-system path through wafer fabrication, chip design, packaging, HBM, processing-unit architectures, halls, data centers, orbital scenarios, model execution, and evaluation.
- Added 210 connected lessons across 57 branches, 14 guided paths, 21 numerical workbench labs, and 73 dated milestones while retaining every v1.0.0 lesson and journey.
- Added the Communication Theory of Computation Realization (CRG) bridge: a source-labeled seven-boundary graph from computation intent through architecture, chip design, physical build, system operation, and evidence.
- Added formal KaTeX/MathML equations, typed relationship navigation, URL state, architecture specimen comparisons, and explicit limits around conceptual geometry, vendor values, scaling claims, and Astra.

The release treats the expanded topics as connected constraints rather than isolated chapters: design choices shape process windows; process yield and package routing shape usable HBM; memory, power, cooling, network and capacity constraints shape hall-level throughput; and measured workload behavior closes the loop back to model progress. Each addition keeps the atlas’s original emphasis on mechanisms, equations, assumptions, interfaces, tradeoffs, checks, and primary references.

## Validation

`npm test` passes the TypeScript check, production build, curriculum and scene validator, deep numerical invariants, and portable asset/license checks. Browser QA covered the formal equations, CRG bridge and process route, numerical workbench, Global and Compare views, the deployed atlas route, and *The Ascent*. The production bundle emits a large-chunk warning because the explanatory 3D/data payload is intentionally rich; the generated static build remains self-contained.

The atlas is an educational model. CRG’s approximately 55-year computation-to-realization interval is labeled as a Semi AI Foundry research claim; it is not presented as an independent chronology, a universal chip-design theorem, or evidence that AGI has been achieved. The atlas does not disclose GPT-6 Astra’s private architecture or deployed hardware.
