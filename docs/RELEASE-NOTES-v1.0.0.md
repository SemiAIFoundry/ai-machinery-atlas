# AI Machinery Atlas v1.0.0

**[Live demo](https://semiaifoundry.com/ai-atlas/)** · **[Changelog](https://github.com/SemiAIFoundry/ai-machinery-atlas/blob/v1.0.0/CHANGELOG.md)**

First public release, September 6, 2026. This edition connects AI machinery through 97 lessons, 26 procedural 3D branches, five learning paths, seven numerical labs, and 41 historical milestones. The Ascent companion film includes locally hosted scripts and generated narration.

## Release contents

- An interactive 3D atlas with a component field guide, scientific explanations, contextual specifications, and primary references.
- A standalone cinematic companion: **[Watch The Ascent](https://semiaifoundry.com/ai-atlas/ascent.html)**.
- Live demo, versioned source and static downloads, and citation metadata.
- A shared version policy for future software, scientific-model, and educational-content updates.

Original source and educational content are available under MIT, copyright **Semi AI Foundry, LLC**. Third-party licenses and attribution remain separate.

## Validation and scope

`npm test` passes TypeScript and the production build; curriculum and scene coverage across all 97 lessons; numerical invariants; root and subdirectory asset resolution; film script parsing; and exact license-text checksums. These automated checks do not constitute browser UI or accessibility certification. The geometry and numerical labs are explanatory; the atlas does not disclose Astra's private architecture or establish an AGI date.
