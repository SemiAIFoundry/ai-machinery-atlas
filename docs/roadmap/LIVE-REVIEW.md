# Live atlas review — 7 September 2026

Read-only product review of https://semiaifoundry.com/ai-atlas/ in desktop Chrome. The reviewer navigated lessons and process controls in a separate research tab. No quiz answers, publication actions, or product edits were made. Ordinary navigation may record visits in that browser's local learning state.

This was a targeted inspection, not an exhaustive browser test, a physical-phone test, a performance benchmark, or a learner study. Source audits provide broader quantitative coverage.

| Surface inspected | Observed behavior | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| GPU die, Learn | Coherent dark three-panel layout, selectable exploded package, clear component identity, sources, an objective and a voltage/power example. | A substantial, usable foundation with a consistent visual identity. |
| GPU die, description | Visible directions include “In the exploded view, show functional regions…” and “The two dies should reconnect…” | Authoring directions remain in learner-facing text. The curriculum audit checks their wider prevalence. |
| Executable software navigation band | Expanded groups include Data-center physical systems, Data-center operations, Global AI infrastructure and Frontier infrastructure. | Confirms the source taxonomy mismatch is visible to learners. The navigation combines different kinds of scale and places several infrastructure groups under software. |
| Energy bands, Science | After the math font finished loading, the formal carrier equation placed only N_C under the radical and retained a stray closing parenthesis. | Confirms the converter defect found by the equation audit occurs on the live site. This is a semantic rendering problem, not merely typography. |
| Energy bands, 3D | Two thick horizontal slabs float above a spatial grid with carrier markers and no visible energy axis. Nearby prose correctly says the gap is an energy difference and the axis is not physical height. | The accurate explanation and spatial metaphor pull in different directions. Use an explicitly labeled energy diagram, coordinated with real-space structure when needed. This is a pedagogical judgment, not a measured learner misconception. |
| Hybrid-bond route, Learn | Specific discussion of surface condition, die-to-wafer versus wafer-to-wafer, and qualification limits. The numerical pad-overlap example explicitly does not equate overlap to bond yield. | Newer process content demonstrates a good level of scientific qualification and engineering detail. Preserve these distinctions. |
| Hybrid-bond route, Process steps 1–2 | “Create bonding surfaces” and “Planarize and measure” update input/action/output/measurement text. Both show the same small complete HBM-stack structure; the second step does not expose local copper topography, roughness or planarization. | Process text is more specific than the visual mechanism. The source geometry probe supports a wider assessment of scene reuse. |
