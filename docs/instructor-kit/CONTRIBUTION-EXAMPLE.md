# A complete second-author example

The candidate **When completed work survives a restart** adds a narrow checkpoint-commit lesson to the existing infrastructure chapter. Its author is AI agent `/root/process_editor`, working as a separate contributor to the main integration task. This is an agent contribution, not evidence of a human second author or an instructor using the material.

The package demonstrates the full content path:

1. [Lesson record](second-author-lesson.json): outcome, mechanism, worked example, prerequisite/related links, source references, assumptions and a three-option knowledge check in the current `RecordEntry` shape.
2. [Formal equations](second-author-equations.json): canonical LaTeX plus symbol meanings, units and accessible text. The main identities distinguish durable, volatile, partial and lost work; the energy ratio excludes a zero denominator.
3. [Evidence ledger](second-author-evidence.json): two scoped primary-source claims. The [SCR documentation](https://scr.readthedocs.io/en/latest/users/integration.html) supplies checkpoint validity and restart semantics; the [DOE/LBNL guide](https://datacenters.lbl.gov/sites/default/files/DataCenterMeteringandResourceGuide_02072017.pdf) supplies facility/IT energy boundaries. The numerical fixture is authored rather than attributed to either source.
4. [Executable contracts](scenario-contracts.json): H7 is a complete small operating-hall scenario. Its result is reproduced by the existing model and checked by a separate, hand-derived timeline calculation.
5. [Provenance](second-author-provenance.json), [content snapshot](second-author-review-snapshot.json) and [source-scope event](second-author-review-event.json): exact actor type, checked passages, qualifiers, date and content fingerprint. Source checking and authoring were performed by the same agent. This does not establish independent human review.
6. [Additive integration proposal](lesson-integration-proposal.json): append the record and chapter ID, source and claim records, formal equations and explicit lab routing. It changes no previous lesson or historical notice.

The source-scope review is accepted for the frozen content snapshot because both cited primary passages were read and support their qualified uses, and the independent arithmetic closes. Human specialist review and classroom observation remain unperformed. The review event is a candidate for the curation ledger; append it only after the effective integrated record has the same fingerprint. A changed scope, equation or lab mapping calls for an updated snapshot and review of that change.

The contribution points to the existing **Rack → retained work** experience. The generic lesson lab remains the library because the small H7 timing/rate contract is not entirely configurable through current browser controls. The pack supplies both a printed timeline and a runner. Claiming direct numeric restoration from its URL would be incorrect: the `studio=hall` fragment selects the experience only.

Repository integration still needs its normal generation, prerequisite/source validation, canonical-math validation and a check that the new lesson has an isolatable scene group and opens with the intended experience. The included verifier checks content and scenario behavior; it does not claim those UI checks were performed. The proposed addition takes the corpus beyond its previous fixed count, so validators and current inventory summaries should derive lesson counts instead of treating 224 as a permanent total. Baseline preservation checks for the original 97 lessons remain meaningful.

A future contributor can copy the pattern, replace the outcome and scoped example, and provide a truthful author/reviewer identity. A citation URL, a passing schema check or a proposed review role does not by itself establish source support or human review.
